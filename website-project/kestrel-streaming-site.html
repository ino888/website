About Kestrel

Kestrel started out grinding ranked ladders in competitive gaming before drifting toward something stranger: long, unscripted debates broadcast live to whoever wanted to watch. What began as a side segment between matches slowly became the main event.

These days the stream is a mix of games, current events, and open-mic debates with anyone willing to queue up — regulars, first-time callers, the occasional expert who wandered in from social media. The chat has its own culture, its own jokes, and strong opinions about all of it.
